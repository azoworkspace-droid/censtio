2. INFORMATION YOU ENTER IN THE APP

Depending on the features you use, the App may store information such as:

- Your business or personal name, profile type, job title, country, and address.
- Currency, tax preferences, tax ID type, tax number, and default tax rate.
- Payment details that you choose to place on invoices.
- Client names and contact details that you enter.
- Invoices, quotes, expenses, notes, amounts, dates, and related records.
- A business logo or other images that you choose to import.
- App preferences, onboarding progress, and security settings.

This information is used to provide the App's invoicing, quote, expense, PDF, reporting, and settings features.

3. WHERE YOUR DATA IS STORED

The App is designed to store your workspace data locally on your device by default. We do not send your tax numbers, bank details, names, or invoice content to our analytics system.

If you delete the App or use an in-App delete/reset feature, locally stored data may be removed. You are responsible for keeping any copies you need. Do not rely on the App as your only backup.

4. AI FEATURES

Some optional features use artificial intelligence, including receipt scanning, invoice or document scanning, and voice input.

When you choose to use an AI feature, the image, audio, transcript, or text required to process your request may be sent to our AI service provider, such as Google Gemini. The information is used to return structured suggestions to the App. You should review every AI result before relying on it and should not submit information that you are not authorized to share.

AI results may be incomplete or inaccurate. The App does not provide tax, accounting, legal, or financial advice.

6. ANALYTICS

The App currently records a limited, privacy-safe list of product events locally on the device. These events may include onboarding progress, feature usage, subscription screens, and completed actions.

Analytics events are designed not to include tax numbers, bank details, names, client information, invoice content, or receipt content. If we introduce a remote analytics provider in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy before doing so.
